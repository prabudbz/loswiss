About Us

Building Financial Inclusion Across India

LOSWISS Small Finance Bank was established with the purpose of providing banking services to underserved and unserved segments of society. We are dedicated to supporting individuals, micro-businesses, small enterprises, farmers, and entrepreneurs through reliable financial solutions.

Our institution combines traditional banking values with modern technology to create a seamless and secure banking experience.

Who We Serve

  • Individuals and Families
  • Small Business Owners
  • MSMEs
  • Farmers and Rural Communities
  • Women Entrepreneurs
  • Startups and Professionals

Our Commitment
