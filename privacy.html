Our Commitment to Privacy

At LOSWISS Small Finance Bank, protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal, financial, and other sensitive data is our highest priority. This privacy notice explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal identification information, contact details, financial data, and transaction histories required to provide you with comprehensive banking services and meet regulatory compliance.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to process transactions, manage your accounts, provide customer support, improve our services, and comply with anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) regulations.
