Protecting Your Assets

At LOSWISS Small Finance Bank, we employ state-of-the-art security measures to protect your accounts from fraudulent activities. However, security is a shared responsibility, and your awareness is the first line of defense.

Recognizing Phishing Attempts

Phishing attempts often look like official bank communications. Remember that LOSWISS will never ask you for your passwords, PINs, or OTPs via email, SMS, or phone call. If you receive a suspicious request, do not click on any links and contact us immediately.

Safe Digital Banking Habits

  • Always access internet banking by typing our official URL into your browser.
  • Keep your computer's operating system, browser, and antivirus software up to date.
  • Avoid conducting banking transactions on public Wi-Fi networks.
  • Regularly monitor your account statements for unauthorized transactions.
